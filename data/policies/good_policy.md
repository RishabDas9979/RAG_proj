# Meridian Example Bank: Information Security and Compliance Policy

This is a fictional policy written for demonstration. Meridian Example Bank does not exist.

## 1. Governance
The Chief Compliance Officer is the policy owner. The Board Risk Committee approves this policy. The policy is reviewed annually and after any material regulatory change.

## 2. Access Control
Access to systems follows the principle of least privilege through role-based access control. Each role is documented by the Head of Information Security. Staff receive only the access their role requires.

## 3. Access Review
The Information Security team performs an access review every quarter. System owners must confirm or revoke each access right within 10 business days of receiving the review list. Unreviewed accounts are disabled.

## 4. Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all privileged accounts, administrator consoles and every remote access session.

## 5. Encryption
Customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Data in transit is protected with TLS 1.2 or higher. The Chief Information Security Officer approves all cryptographic standards.

## 6. Data Retention and Disposal
Customer records and transaction records are retained for 7 years after the account closes. Audit logs are retained for 7 years. At the end of the retention period the Records Management team securely destroys the data and files a certificate of disposal.

## 7. Privacy and Consent
The bank collects personal data only with documented customer consent and only for stated purposes. The Data Protection Officer handles customer privacy requests within 30 days.

## 8. Incident Response
The Security Incident Response Team, led by the CISO, investigates every security incident. The bank completes breach notification to the regulator within 72 hours of confirming a breach. A post-incident review is completed within 14 days.

## 9. Business Continuity
The Business Continuity Manager maintains the business continuity and disaster recovery plan. Disaster recovery tests run twice a year. The recovery time objective for core banking is 4 hours.

## 10. Know Your Customer
The Head of Customer Onboarding is responsible for KYC. Customer due diligence includes identity verification, beneficial ownership checks and a risk rating before account opening. Customer profiles are refreshed every year for high-risk customers and every 3 years for others.

## 11. Enhanced Due Diligence
Politically exposed persons and other high-risk customers require enhanced due diligence and senior management approval before onboarding.

## 12. Sanctions Screening
Customers and counterparties are screened against sanctions and watchlists at onboarding and daily afterwards. The Compliance team reviews every potential match.

## 13. Transaction Monitoring
The Money Laundering Reporting Officer owns transaction monitoring. Alerts are reviewed within 5 business days. Each suspicious activity report is filed with the financial intelligence unit within 3 days of the officer's decision.

## 14. Audit Trail
All access to customer data and all case decisions are written to an immutable audit log. Each entry is hash-chained so that tampering is detectable.

## 15. Third-Party Risk
The Procurement and Risk team assesses each vendor and outsourcing arrangement before contract signature and reviews critical vendors every year.

## 16. Training
All staff complete compliance training annually. Staff in financial crime roles complete additional training every 6 months.
