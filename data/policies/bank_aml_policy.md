# Example Bank: AML and KYC Operating Procedure

This is a fictional procedure written for the BankComply demo. It is not a real institution's policy.

## 1. Structuring
Repeated cash deposits just below the reporting threshold within a short period indicate structuring. The analyst must escalate structuring patterns for review by the Compliance Officer.

## 2. Rapid Movement of Funds
Funds that enter an account and leave it within 48 hours in nearly the same amount indicate pass-through or layering activity. The analyst must document the source and destination of the funds.

## 3. High-Risk Jurisdictions
Transactions with counterparties in high-risk jurisdictions require enhanced scrutiny. Activity above 25,000 in total is escalated.

## 4. Watchlist and Sanctions Matches
A strong name match against a sanctions list or watchlist is escalated immediately. A possible match is investigated with additional identifiers before any decision.

## 5. Politically Exposed Persons
Politically exposed persons require enhanced due diligence, including source-of-funds verification and senior approval.

## 6. Unusual Transaction Volume
Activity far above a customer's expected profile requires a source-of-funds review and an updated customer profile.

## 7. Large Cash Transactions
Cash transactions at or above the reporting threshold are reported as required and recorded in the case file.

## 8. Expired or Outdated KYC Records
Customers with an expired identity document or an overdue KYC review must refresh their records. Accounts may be restricted until refreshed.

## 9. Suspicious Activity Reports
The Compliance Officer approves each suspicious activity report. The approver must be a different person from the analyst who prepared the case.
