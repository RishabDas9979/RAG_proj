# Northwind Example Bank: Security Policy (DRAFT)

This is a fictional, intentionally flawed policy used to test PolicyGuard.

## 1. Overview

## 2. Access
Access is granted as needed and should be reviewed periodically. Managers should consider removing access that is no longer required where possible.

## 3. Data Protection
Sensitive data should be protected using reasonable efforts. Some customer data may be encrypted when appropriate.

## 4. Retention
Customer records shall be retained for 5 years.

## 5. Records Management
Customer records are kept for 7 years after account closure, then disposed of securely.

## 6. Incidents
Security incidents are handled promptly by the relevant team. Notification to regulators is made as soon as possible.

## 7. Customer Due Diligence
The bank performs KYC on new customers where possible.

## 8. Monitoring
Transactions are monitored regularly and suspicious activity is reported in a timely manner.

## 9. Training
Staff receive awareness training from time to time.
